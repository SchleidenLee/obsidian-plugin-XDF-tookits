### will 和 be going to
1. **will**
	- 没有计划的/猜测（没有消息来源）
2. **be going to** 
	- 有计划的（有消息来源）

你：Do you want to have lunch with me？

- Sorry, I will have lunch with my roommates. 
- Sorry, I am going to have lunch with my roommates.

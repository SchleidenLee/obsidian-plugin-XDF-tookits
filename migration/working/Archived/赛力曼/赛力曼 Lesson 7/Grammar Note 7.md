
## 1️⃣ Passive Voice — be done（被动语态）

### ✅ 基本结构

> 主语 + be + 过去分词 (done)

### ✅ 构成变化（时态变化看 be）

| 时态    | 结构                     | 例句                                   |
| ----- | ---------------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| 一般现在时 | am / is / are + done   | The homework is finished.            |
| 一般过去时 | was / were + done      | The meeting was canceled.            |
| 一般将来时 | will be + done         | The work will be completed tomorrow. |
| 现在完成时 | has / have been + done | The problem has been solved.         |

---

### ✅ 什么时候用被动？

1. 不知道动作执行者  
   > My bike was stolen.

2. 不重要谁做的  
   > English is spoken worldwide.

3. 强调动作承受者  
   > The bridge was built in 1990.

---

### ✅ 主动变被动步骤

主动：
> They canceled the meeting.

被动：
> The meeting was canceled (by them).

步骤：
1. 宾语 → 主语  
2. 动词变 be done  
3. 主语变 by + 人（可省略）

## 2️⃣ Post-modifiers（后置定语）

后置定语 = 放在名词后面修饰名词的成分。

---

## A. 定语从句 (Relative Clause)

### ✅ 结构

> 名词 + 关系词 + 句子

### ✅ 常见关系词

| 关系词 | 用法 |
|--------|------|
| who | 指人 |
| whom | 指人（宾格） |
| which | 指物 |
| that | 指人/物 |
| where | 指地点 |
| when | 指时间 |

---

### ✅ 例句

- The girl **who is singing** is my sister.  
- The book **that I bought** is interesting.  
- The city **where I live** is busy.

---

## B. 分词作后置定语 (Participle as Post-modifier)

### 1️⃣ 现在分词（doing）—— 主动

> 名词 + doing

表示“正在”或“主动”

- The man **standing there** is my teacher.
- Students **studying abroad** face challenges.

---

### 2️⃣ 过去分词（done）—— 被动

> 名词 + done

表示“被动”或“完成”

- The books **written by him** are popular.
- The problem **discussed yesterday** is important.

---

### 3️⃣ 不定式作后置定语

> 名词 + to do

表示“将来”或“目的”

- I have a lot of work **to do**.
- She is the first student **to arrive**.

---

---

## 🔎 三种后置定语对比

| 形式 | 含义 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| who is standing | 完整句 | The man who is standing there |
| standing | 主动简化 | The man standing there |
| written | 被动简化 | The book written by him |
| to do | 将来 | The work to finish |



### 定语从句 补充
*The girl is singing*
*I bought the book yesterday.*
1. 把**被修饰名词**提前
   *The girl.      is singing*
   *The book    I bought yesterday*
2. **被修饰名词后**加入关系词
   *The girl <font color="#2DC26B">who</font> is singing*
   *The book <font color="#2DC26B">that</font> I bought yesterday*

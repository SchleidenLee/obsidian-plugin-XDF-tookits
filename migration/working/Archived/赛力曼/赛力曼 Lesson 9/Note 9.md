# 被动语态
> [!note] 
> 结构：**be + done** 

*She takes his son to school*
1. **宾语**变**主语**
   *his son     takes.   her   to school
2. do 变 be done，施动者前加 by
   *His son is taken to school by her.*

# 后置定语
固定翻译：xxxx 的 xx
## 定语从句
> [!note] 
> 句子做定语，修饰名词 

She took bus to work 
1. 把要修饰的名词放最前面
2. 在被修饰名词后加入关系词
    The girl who took bus to work
## 分词做后置定语
- 主动关系：doing
  The girl taking bus to work
- 被动关系：done
  The boy taken to school by her mother

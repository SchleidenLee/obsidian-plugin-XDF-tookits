## 一、核心语法

### 1️⃣ 形容词 + to do 结构
*   **结构**：`主语 + be动词 + 形容词 + to do`
*   **用法**：用于描述主语的特征或状态，不定式 `to do` 与主语存在逻辑上的动宾关系。
*   **例句**：
    *   The homework is difficult to do.
    *   This burger is delicious to eat.


### 2️⃣ 介词 `like` 表示“像”
*   **结构**：`A + be动词 + like + B` 或 `动词 + like + B`
*   **用法**：表示比较或相似，`like` 在这里是介词，后面接名词或代词。
*   **例句**：
    *   He looks like his father.
    *   She sings like a professional.


### 3️⃣ `be able to` 与 `can`
*   **结构**：`主语 + be able to + 动词原形`
*   **用法**：表示“能够”，与 `can` 同义，但有时态变化（如：was able to, will be able to）。
*   **例句**：
    *   I am able to speak three languages. = I can speak three languages.
    *   She will be able to drive next year.

## 二、固定搭配/用法
*   **deal with** - 处理，解决 - We need to learn how to deal with stress.
*   **care about** - 关心，在乎 - He really cares about his family.
*   **have an influence on** - 对...有影响 - Parents have a great influence on their children.
*   **have an effect on** - 对...有影响 (与 `influence` 近义) - The weather has an effect on my mood.
*   **be grateful for** - 为...而感激 - I am grateful for your help.
*   **plenty of** - 大量的，充足的 (后接可数或不可数名词) - We have plenty of time.
*   **save money** - 存钱，省钱 - It’s important to save money for the future.
*   **extended family** - 大家庭 (包括祖父母、叔伯、堂兄妹等) - In some cultures, the extended family lives together.
*   **well-known** - 出名的，众所周知的 - He is a well-known writer.

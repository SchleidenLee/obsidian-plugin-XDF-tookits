## 二、重点语法整理

### 1. 被动语态（Passive Voice）

- I do homework. → Homework is done.
    
- I divide the cake into four parts. → The cake is divided into four parts.
    

---

### 2. 主谓一致（Subject–Verb Agreement）

- 不可数名词作主语 → 谓语用三单  
    ✔ Information helps me.
    
- 并列主语 → 谓语用复数  
    ✔ Toast and cereal are good for health.
    

---

### 3. find + 宾语 + adj + to do

- I find homework difficult to do.
    
- She finds the challenge easy to handle.
    

---

### 4. it 作形式主语

- It is easy for me to do my homework.
    
- It is difficult for pupils to use this oven.
    

---

### 5. 名词数量修饰词

- many + 可数名词复数
    
- much + 不可数名词
    
- a few / a little（肯定）
    
- few / little（否定）
    

---

### 6. some / any 用法

- 肯定句：some  
    ✔ I have some cereal.
    
- 疑问句 / 否定句：any  
    ✔ Do you have any cereal?
    
- 请求或期待肯定回答：some  
    ✔ Would you like some bread?

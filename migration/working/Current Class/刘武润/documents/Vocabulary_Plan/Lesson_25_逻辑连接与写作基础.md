## ⚡ 核心语法攻坚：对比与比较结构
> **痛点**: 不会表达事物之间的异同
> **结构**: 
> 1. A is similar to B (A 与 B 相似)
> 2. A is different from B (A 与 B 不同)
> 3. A is + 形容词比较级 + than B
> 4. while / whereas 表示对比

**典型例句**:
- Life in the city **is different from** life in the countryside.
- English **is similar to** French in some ways.
- She is **taller than** her sister.
- This book is **more interesting than** that one.
- Some people like coffee, **while** others prefer tea. (对比)
- He is rich, **whereas** his brother is poor. (对比)

**比较级规则**:
- 单音节词: +er (tall → taller)
- 双音节以 y 结尾: 变 y 为 i + er (happy → happier)
- 多音节词: more + 原级 (interesting → more interesting)
- 不规则: good → better, bad → worse

**课堂指令建议**:
给出两样事物 (如 city vs countryside, phone vs computer)，要求学员用 3 个不同的比较句型描述它们的异同。

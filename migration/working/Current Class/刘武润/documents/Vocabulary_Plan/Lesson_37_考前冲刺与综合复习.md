## ⚡ 核心语法攻坚：写作句型多样化训练
> **目标**: 避免句式单一，提升写作分数
> **句型结构**: 
> 1. 简单句 (主谓宾)
> 2. 并列句 (and/but/so 连接)
> 3. 复合句 (定语从句/名词性从句/状语从句)
> 4. 非谓语动词结构
> 5. 被动语态句
> 6. 倒装句 (了解即可)
> 7. 强调句 (了解即可)

**典型句型练习**:
- 简单句: Technology has changed our lives.
- 并列句: Technology has changed our lives, **but** it has also created new problems.
- 复合句: **Although** technology has brought many benefits, **there are** some people **who argue that** it causes more harm than good.
- 非谓语:**Compared with** the past, modern life is much more convenient.
- 被动:Many problems **have been caused** by the overuse of technology.
- 倒装:**Not only does** technology improve efficiency, **but it also** saves time.
- 强调:**It is** technology **that** has revolutionized the way we communicate.

**课堂指令建议**:
给出一个简单句，要求学员用 7 种不同句型改写；给出一篇学员之前的作文，要求找出可以升级句式的句子并修改。

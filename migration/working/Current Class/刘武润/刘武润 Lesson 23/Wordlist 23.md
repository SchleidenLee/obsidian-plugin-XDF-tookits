
### 一、核心语法

#### 1️⃣ 连词用法：despite vs. although
- **结构**：
    - despite + 名词/名词短语
    - although + 句子
- **用法**：两者都表示“虽然，尽管”，但后接成分不同。
- **例句**：
    - Despite the rain, we went out.
    - Although it rained, we went out.


#### 2️⃣ 动词不定式结构：force sb. to do
- **结构**：force + 宾语 + to do
- **用法**：表示“强迫某人做某事”。
- **例句**：The boss forced him to work overtime.


#### 3️⃣ 动词搭配：avoid doing
- **结构**：avoid + doing (动名词)
- **用法**：表示“避免做某事”。
- **例句**：You should avoid making the same mistake.


#### 4️⃣ 目的状语：in order to do
- **结构**：in order to + 动词原形
- **用法**：表示“为了做某事”，引导目的状语。
- **例句**：He studies hard in order to pass the exam.


### 二、固定搭配/用法

- **be different from**
    - 意思：和......不一样
    - 例句：My opinion is different from yours.
- **depend on**
    - 意思：依靠，取决于
    - 例句：The price depends on the quality.
- **avoid doing**
    - 意思：避免做某事
    - 例句：You should avoid eating too much sugar.
- **force sb. to do**
    - 意思：强迫某人做某事
    - 例句：Don't force me to go.
- **in order to do**
    - 意思：为了做某事
    - 例句：She left early in order to catch the bus.

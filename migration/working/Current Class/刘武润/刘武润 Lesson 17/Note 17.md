## 一、核心语法

### 1️⃣ 比较级
*   **结构**：形容词/副词 + `-er` + `than`
*   **用法**：用于两者之间的比较，表示"更……"。
*   **例句**：
    *   I am **smarter than** you.
    *   I run **faster than** you.
*   **易错点**：
    *   ❌ I am smart than you.
    *   ✔ I am **smarter than** you. (比较级需加 `-er`)

### 2️⃣ `how` 引导的特殊疑问句
*   **结构**：`How` + 量词 + 其他成分？
*   **用法**：`how` 与量词搭配，用于询问数量、程度、频率等。
*   **例句**：
    *   How long will you stay? (询问时长)
    *   How much water is there? (询问不可数数量)
    *   How many books do you have? (询问可数数量)
*   **易错点**：
    *   ❌ How many water do you need?
    *   ✔ How **much** water do you need? (`water` 不可数，用 `much`)

### 3️⃣ 情态动词
*   **结构**：情态动词 + 动词原形
*   **用法**：表示能力、许可、必要、推测等含义，本身有词义。
*   **例句**：
    *   You **can** swim here. (表示许可/能力)
    *   We **must** finish the work. (表示必要)
    *   It **might** rain later. (表示可能性)
*   **易错点**：
    *   ❌ He can **to speak** English.
    *   ✔ He can **speak** English. (情态动词后接动词原形)

### 4️⃣ `used to do`
*   **结构**：`used to` + 动词原形
*   **用法**：表示过去常常做某事或存在某种状态，但现在已不如此。
*   **例句**：I **used to get up** late, but now I get up early.
*   **易错点**：
    *   ❌ I am used to get up early. (混淆 `used to do` 和 `be used to doing`)
    *   ✔ I **used to get up** late. (过去习惯)

### 5️⃣ `keep doing`
*   **结构**：`keep` + 动词-ing形式
*   **用法**：表示"持续做某事"或"坚持做某事"。
*   **例句**：**Keep practicing**, and you will improve.
*   **易错点**：
    *   ❌ Keep **to practice** every day.
    *   ✔ Keep **practicing** every day. (`keep` 后接动词-ing形式)

### 6️⃣ `be about to do`
*   **结构**：`be about to` + 动词原形
*   **用法**：表示"即将、马上要做某事"，强调动作迫在眉睫。
*   **例句**：The movie **is about to start**. Hurry up!
*   **易错点**：
    *   ❌ I am about to **watching** TV.
    *   ✔ I am about to **watch** TV. (`be about to` 后接动词原形)

## 二、固定搭配/用法

*   **take part in** - 参与
    *   例句：Many athletes **take part in** the race.
*   **give up** - 放弃
    *   例句：Don't **give up** your dream easily.
*   **make a difference** - 有作用，产生影响
    *   例句：Your help can **make a big difference**.
*   **take a break** - 休息一下
    *   例句：Let's **take a break** after an hour's work.
*   **pay attention to** - 注意，关注
    *   例句：Please **pay attention to** the teacher.

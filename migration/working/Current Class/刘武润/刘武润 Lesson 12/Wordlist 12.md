# Grammar & Vocabulary Notes

---

## 一、核心语法

### 1️⃣ 现在进行时（Present Continuous）

**结构：**
> be (am / is / are) + doing  

**用法：**
- 表示“正在发生”的动作

**例句：**
- She is playing hockey.  
- He is practicing listening.  
- They are moving rapidly.  

**常见标志词：**
- now / look / listen  

---

### 2️⃣ 被动语态（Passive Voice）

#### 基本结构：
> be + done (+ by sb.)

**例句：**
- Homework is done by me.  
- The book is written by him.  

---

#### ⭐ 进行时被动（重点！）
> be + being + done  

**例句：**
- The game is being played.  
- The house is being built.  

👉 常见错误：
- ❌ is play  
- ❌ is playing（主动）  
- ✔ is being played  

---

## 二、常用结构 & 搭配

### 1️⃣ help 结构

- help sb. do sth.  
- help sb. with sth.  

**例句：**
- She helps me do homework.  
- She helps me with homework.  

---

### 2️⃣ be based on

**意思：** 基于  

**例句：**
- The movie is based on a true story.  

---

### 3️⃣ 情绪表达

- be angry with sb.（对某人生气）  
- be happy with sth.（对某事满意）  

**例句：**
- He is angry with his friend.  
- She is happy with the result.  

---

### 4️⃣ instead of

**意思：** 代替  

**例句：**
- Drink water instead of soda.  

---

### 5️⃣ be important to

**意思：** 对……重要  

**例句：**
- English is important to students.  

---

## 三、介词结构（重点理解）

### 1️⃣ from...to...

**意思：** 从……到……  

- from A to B  

**例句：**
- We study from morning to night.  

---

### 2️⃣ between / between…and…

**意思：** 在……之间  

**例句：**
- The shop is between the bank and the school.

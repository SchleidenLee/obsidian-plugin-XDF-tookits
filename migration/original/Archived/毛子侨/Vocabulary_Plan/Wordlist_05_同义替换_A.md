# 📚 毛子侨·6 分词汇冲刺计划 (Optimized)
## Wordlist 05: 同义替换篇 (A) - 逻辑信号词与万能动词升级

> **⚠️ 优化说明**：剔除 `and`, `but`, `so` 等基础词，直接上**高阶逻辑词**。  
> **目标**：掌握雅思阅读和写作中最核心的同义替换链，提升 Paraphrasing 能力。

---

## A 组：复健区 (15 词) - 逻辑连接词进阶
*别只会用 `because`, `but`, `so`。这些升级词能让你的写作瞬间提升一个档次。*

| # | Basic Word | **Upgrade Words** (Formal/Academic) | Example Sentence | Type |
|:-:|:-----------|:------------------------------------|:-----------------|:-----|
| 1 | **because** | **due to**, **owing to**, **in view of**, **given that** | **Due to** the rain, the match was cancelled. | 👁️ 识别 |
| 2 | **so** | **therefore**, **thus**, **consequently**, **hence**, **accordingly** | He was late; **consequently**, he missed the train. | 👁️ 识别 |
| 3 | **but** | **however**, **nevertheless**, **nonetheless**, **yet**, **still** | It was raining; **nevertheless**, we went out. | 👁️ 识别 |
| 4 | **although** | **while**, **whereas**, **despite**, **in spite of**, **notwithstanding** | **Despite** the difficulty, he succeeded. | 👁️ 识别 |
| 5 | **and** | **furthermore**, **moreover**, **in addition**, **besides**, **additionally** | The plan is cheap; **moreover**, it is effective. | 👁️ 识别 |
| 6 | **also** | **likewise**, **similarly**, **correspondingly**, **equally** | She studies hard; **likewise**, her brother does. | 👁️ 识别 |
| 7 | **for example** | **for instance**, **to illustrate**, **specifically**, **notably** | Many fruits, **such as** apples, are rich in Vitamin C. | 👁️ 识别 |
| 8 | **in conclusion** | **to sum up**, **overall**, **in brief**, **to conclude**, **ultimately** | **Overall**, the benefits outweigh the drawbacks. | 👁️ 识别 |
| 9 | **if** | **provided that**, **as long as**, **unless**, **in case**, **lest** | You can go out **provided that** you finish homework. | 👁️ 识别 |
| 10 | **now** | **currently**, **at present**, **nowadays**, **these days**, **contemporarily** | **Currently**, the project is under review. | 👁️ 识别 |
| 11 | **before** | **previously**, **prior to**, **formerly**, **earlier**, **antecedently** | **Previously**, he worked in London. | 👁️ 识别 |
| 12 | **after** | **subsequently**, **later**, **following**, **eventually**, **ultimately** | He graduated and **subsequently** found a job. | 👁️ 识别 |
| 13 | **at the same time** | **meanwhile**, **simultaneously**, **concurrently**, **in the meantime** | **Meanwhile**, the team was working on another project. | 👁️ 识别 |
| 14 | **very** | **extremely**, **highly**, **remarkably**, **exceptionally**, **exceedingly** | The test was **exceptionally** difficult. | 👁️ 识别 |
| 15 | **really** | **truly**, **indeed**, **actually**, **in fact**, **virtually** | It is **indeed** a great opportunity. | 👁️ 识别 |

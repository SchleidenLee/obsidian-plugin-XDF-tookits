# 五大基本句型
### 1. 主系表  （主语补语）
*He is a teacher.*   名词
*she is very smart.*    形容词
### 2. 主谓宾
谓语动词：及物动词 实义动词 
*they handled the difficult problem.*
*Tom earned the phone he want.*
### 3. 主谓
*I run.* 
*he deals with the friend he hates*
### 4. 主谓宾 宾
*I bought my son a car.*
*I gave my sister a computer.*
### 5. 主谓宾 宾补
*I will make you happy.*
*Cold food will make people sick*
*I will make you my best friend.*

# Animal Migration

Every year, many animals **begin** a long **journey** to find better living conditions. This **movement** from one place to another is called **migration**.

Birds are famous for their seasonal **travel**. When winter **arrives**, they **fly** to warmer areas. They **search for** food and safe **places** to live. In spring, they **return** to their **original** homes to **breed** and raise their **offspring**.

Some animals **migrate** to **avoid** **dangerous** weather. Others **move** to **locate** new **sources** of food. This **behaviour** helps them **survive** in **changing** environments.

During migration, animals **use** their **instinct** to find the way. Young animals **learn** this **skill** from older ones. It is **essential** for the **continuation** of their **species**.

Scientists **study** animal migration to **understand** how **creatures** **adapt** to different habitats. This **knowledge** can **help** **protect** **endangered** animals.

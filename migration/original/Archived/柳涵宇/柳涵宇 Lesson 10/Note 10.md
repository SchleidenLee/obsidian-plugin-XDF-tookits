## 一、核心语法

### 1️⃣ among 与 between 的区别
*   **结构**：
    *   `among + 复数名词/集合名词` (三者或以上)
    *   `between + 复数名词` (两者之间)
*   **用法**：表示“在…中间/之间”。
*   **例句**：
    *   The secret was shared **among** the five friends.
    *   There is a strong bond **between** the two brothers.
*   **易错点**：
    *   ❌ There is a disagreement **among** the two teams.
    *   ✔ There is a disagreement **between** the two teams.

### 2️⃣ despite 的用法
*   **结构**：`Despite + 名词/名词短语/动名词, + 主句` 或 `主句 + despite + 名词/名词短语/动名词`。
*   **用法**：表示“尽管”，引导让步状语。
*   **例句**：**Despite** the heavy rain, the match continued.
*   **易错点**：
    *   ❌ **Despite of** the difficulty, he succeeded.
    *   ✔ **Despite** the difficulty, he succeeded. (注意：despite 后不加 of)

### 3️⃣ like 与 unlike 的用法
*   **结构**：`Like/Unlike + 名词/代词, + 主句`。
*   **用法**：用作介词，表示“像…”或“不像…”，用于比较。
*   **例句**：
    *   **Like** his father, he is very tall.
    *   **Unlike** yesterday, today is sunny.
*   **易错点**：
    *   ❌ He looks **like** his father does. (like 是介词，后接名词性成分，不引导从句)
    *   ✔ He looks **like** his father. 或 He looks **as if** he is his father.

### 4️⃣ lead to 与 lead sb. to do
*   **结构**：
    *   `某事/某物 + lead to + 名词/动名词` (导致…结果)
    *   `某事/某物 + lead + 某人 + to do sth.` (导致某人做某事)
*   **用法**：表示“导致”或“引导某人做某事”。
*   **例句**：
    *   Poor diet can **lead to** health problems.
    *   His words **led me to believe** he was honest.
*   **易错点**：
    *   ❌ Hard work leads to **succeed**.
    *   ✔ Hard work leads to **success**. (to 是介词，后接名词)

### 5️⃣ a number of 的用法
*   **结构**：`A number of + 复数名词 + 复数动词`。
*   **用法**：表示“许多，大量”，相当于 many。
*   **例句**：**A number of** students **are** absent today.
*   **易错点**：
    *   ❌ A number of books **is** on the table.
    *   ✔ A number of books **are** on the table. (谓语动词用复数)

## 二、固定搭配/用法
*   **lead to**：导致，造成 (某事发生)。例句：Smoking **leads to** various diseases.
*   **lead sb. to do sth.**：导致/引导某人做某事。例句：Her encouragement **led him to try** again.
*   **rather than**：而不是。例句：I prefer tea **rather than** coffee.
*   **a number of**：许多，大量。例句：**A number of** factors influence the decision.
*   **complain about**：抱怨…。例句：Customers often **complain about** the high prices.

# 五大基本句型
### 主谓
*I run*
*I exercise in a park*
### 主谓宾
*I do my homework.*
### 主系表
**系动词**：be 
保持类：keep remain
变化类 go / become
感官类 ：
**描述主语的状态或身份**
表语：形容词 名词 介词短语 
### 主谓宾宾
*I bought a car for my friend.* = *I bought my friend a car.*
### 主谓宾 宾补
*I want to make you happy.*
*I want to make you my best friend.*
*Please keep your electronic devices off*

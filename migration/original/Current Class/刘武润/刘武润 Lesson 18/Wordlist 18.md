### 一、核心语法

#### 1️⃣ be about to do
- **结构**：主语 + be about to + 动词原形
- **用法**：表示"即将、正要做某事"，强调动作马上发生。
- **例句**：The plane is about to take off.
- **易错点**：
    - ❌ 错误：I am about to going to school.
    - ✔ 正确：I am about to go to school. (to后接动词原形)

#### 2️⃣ used to do vs. be used to doing
- **结构**：
    - used to do：主语 + used to + 动词原形
    - be used to doing：主语 + be used to + 动名词/名词
- **用法**：
    - used to do：表示过去常常做某事，但现在不做了。
    - be used to doing：表示现在习惯于做某事。
- **例句**：
    - I used to get up late, but now I am used to getting up early.
- **易错点**：
    - ❌ 错误：I am used to get up early.
    - ✔ 正确：I am used to getting up early. (to是介词，后接动名词)
    - ❌ 错误：I used to getting up late.
    - ✔ 正确：I used to get up late. (to是不定式符号，后接动词原形)

#### 3️⃣ suggest
- **结构**：suggest + doing / suggest + that从句 (从句用虚拟语气 should do)
- **用法**：表示"建议"。
- **例句**：He suggested taking a break. / He suggested that we (should) take a break.
- **易错点**：
    - ❌ 错误：He suggested me to take a break.
    - ✔ 正确：He suggested (my) taking a break. 或 He suggested that I (should) take a break. (suggest后不接sb. to do)

#### 4️⃣ make a difference
- **结构**：make a difference (to...)
- **用法**：表示"有作用，有影响，使不同"。
- **例句**：Your help made a big difference to my project.
- **易错点**：
    - ❌ 错误：Your help made difference.
    - ✔ 正确：Your help made a difference. (固定搭配，a不能省略)

#### 5️⃣ tired vs. tiring
- **结构**：
    - tired：主语 + be/feel tired
    - tiring：主语 + be tiring
- **用法**：
    - tired：用来形容人"感到疲惫的"。
    - tiring：用来形容事物"令人疲惫的"。
- **例句**：I am very tired because the work is tiring.
- **易错点**：
    - ❌ 错误：I am very tiring after the long walk.
    - ✔ 正确：I am very tired after the long walk. (人用-ed)
    - ❌ 错误：The journey is tired.
    - ✔ 正确：The journey is tiring. (物用-ing)

### 二、固定搭配/用法

- **pay attention (to)**：注意
    - 例句：Please pay attention to the teacher.
- **ask sb. to do**：叫某人做某事
    - 例句：My mom asked me to clean my room.
- **decide to do**：决定做某事
    - 例句：I decided to apply for the job.
- **to my surprise**：出乎我的意料
    - 例句：To my surprise, he passed the exam.
- **disagree with**：不同意
    - 例句：I disagree with your opinion.
- **apply for**：申请
    - 例句：She applied for a new job.
- **around the world = around the globe**：全世界
    - 例句：People around the world love music.
- **take part in = participate in**：参加
    - 例句：Many students took part in the competition.
- **no longer**：再也不
    - 例句：He no longer lives here.
- **rely on**：依赖
    - 例句：You can rely on me.
- **be related to**：和...有关
    - 例句：This issue is related to the economy.
- **enhance = improve**：增强，提高
    - 例句：Reading can enhance your vocabulary.

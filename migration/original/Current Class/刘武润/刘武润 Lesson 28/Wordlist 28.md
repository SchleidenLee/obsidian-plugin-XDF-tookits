
### 2️⃣ 动词 `involve` 的用法

- **结构**：`involve + 名词/动名词 (doing)` 或 `be involved in + 名词/动名词`
- **用法**：
    - `involve` (及物动词)：包含，涉及。
    - `be involved in`：参与，被卷入。
- **例句**：
    - The project **involves** a lot of research. (这个项目涉及很多研究。)
    - He **is involved in** the construction of the new building. (他参与了新大楼的建设。)
- **易错点**：
    - ❌ 混淆主动和被动：`He involves in the project.` (错误，应为被动)
    - ✔ 正确用法：`He is involved in the project.`
    - ❌ 忘记介词 `in`：`He is involved the project.`
    - ✔ 正确用法：`He is involved in the project.`

### 3️⃣ 动词 `try` 与 `test` 的区别

- **结构**：`try to do` / `try doing` / `test + 名词`
- **用法**：
    - `try`：尝试做某事（`try to do` 尽力去做；`try doing` 试着做做看）。
    - `test`：测试、检验（某物的性能、质量等）。
- **例句**：
    - I will **try to** finish the report today. (我会尽力今天完成报告。)
    - You should **try** using a different method. (你应该试试用不同的方法。)
    - We need to **test** the new software. (我们需要测试这个新软件。)
- **易错点**：
    - ❌ 用 `test` 表示“尝试做某事”：`I will test to finish it.`
    - ✔ 正确用法：`I will try to finish it.`
    - ❌ 用 `try` 表示“测试产品”：`We need to try the new software.` (虽然可以，但 `test` 更准确)
    - ✔ 更精确用法：`We need to test the new software.`

## 二、固定搭配/用法

- **be described as**：被描述为
    - *例句*：The invention **was described as** a breakthrough.
- **be regarded as**：被视为，被认为是
    - *例句*：He **is regarded as** a leading scientist.
- **be considered (as)**：被认为是 (as 可省略)
    - *例句*：This **is considered** (as) a great achievement.
- **be involved in**：参与，卷入
    - *例句*：Many people **are involved in** this controversy.
- **negative effect**：负面影响
    - *例句*：Screen time can have a **negative effect** on sleep.
- **aim / target / goal**：目标 (三者常可互换)
    - *例句*：Our main **goal** is to achieve the sales **target**.

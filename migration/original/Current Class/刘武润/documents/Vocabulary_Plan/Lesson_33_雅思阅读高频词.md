## ⚡ 核心语法攻坚：阅读长难句分析
> **痛点**: 看到长句子就懵，找不到主谓宾
> **策略**: 
> 1. 先找谓语动词
> 2. 确定主语和宾语
> 3. 识别从句和修饰成分
> 4. 暂时忽略插入语

**典型长难句分析**:
- The study, **which was conducted by researchers from Harvard University**, **suggests** that regular exercise **can significantly reduce** the risk of heart disease.
  - 主语: The study
  - 谓语: suggests
  - 宾语从句: that regular exercise can significantly reduce...
  - 插入语 (非限制性定语从句): which was conducted by...

- Despite **the fact that** technology has brought numerous benefits to our daily lives, **there are** some experts **who argue that** excessive reliance on devices **may have** detrimental effects on social skills.
  - 让步状语: Despite the fact that...
  - 主句: there are some experts
  - 定语从句: who argue that...
  - 宾语从句: that excessive reliance... may have...

**课堂指令建议**:
给出 3-5 个长难句，要求学员用括号法标出主谓宾；给出中文翻译，要求学员在英文原句中找到对应信息的位置。

It was first (2) ______ by Sun Yat-sen and later became popular nationwide. Many people (3) ______ the design to a combination of Chinese and Western styles. In recent years, efforts have been made to (4) ______ and restore traditional costumes. Some museums focus on the (5) ______ of these valuable garments.  

When we talk about traditional costumes, we cannot ignore the (6) ______ role they play in stage performances. Actors (7) ______ in elaborate costumes often astonish the audience with their (8) ______ details. The more attention we pay to these cultural treasures, (9) ______ we can appreciate their beauty. As a result, more and more young people are (10) ______ an interest in traditional clothing.

5. A. creation   B. conservation C. conclusion  D. communication
6. A. critical   B. critic      C. criminal    D. curious
7. A. dressing   B. dressed    C. dress       D. to dress
8. A. quickly    B. quietly     C. rapid       D. delicate

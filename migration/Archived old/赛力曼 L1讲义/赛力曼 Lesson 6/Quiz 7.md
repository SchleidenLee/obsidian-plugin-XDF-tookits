
## Part 1 词性选择（形容词 / 副词）

选择正确形式填空：

1. It is a ______ company. (modern / modernly)
    
2. She studies very ______. (hard / hardly)
    
3. This is a very ______ problem. (practical / practically)
    
4. He works as a ______ tutor. (qualified / qualification)
    
5. Online shopping is very ______. (convenient / conveniently)
    
6. This is an ______ issue. (acceptable / acceptably)

## Part 3 可数 & 不可数

判断正误（√ / ×），并改正错误：

1. I have many information.
    
2. She bought a piece of toast.
    
3. There are many carrot in the fridge.
    
4. We need much flour.
    
5. He eats chicken every day.
    
6. There are three chickens in the rural area.

## Part 4 现在完成时

用 have / has done 形式完成句子：

1. I ______ (live) in an urban area for 5 years.
    
2. She ______ (work) in this company since 2020.
    
3. The company ______ (produce) vegetarian food for many years.
    
4. He ______ (retire), so he has more time now.
    
5. We ______ (focus) on this issue recently.
    

改错：

6. I have finished my degree last year.
    
7. She has delivered the food yesterday.

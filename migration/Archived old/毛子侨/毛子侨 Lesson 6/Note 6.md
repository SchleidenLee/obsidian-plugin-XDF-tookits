
## 一、核心语法

### 1️⃣ It is time sb. did
- **结构**：It is time + 主语 + 动词过去式
- **用法**：表示“是某人做某事的时候了”，带有建议或催促的语气，暗含“早该做了”。
- **例句**：
  - It is time you **started** your homework.
  - It is time we **left** for the airport.
- **易错点**：
  - ❌ It is time you **start** your homework. (误用动词原形)
  - ✔ It is time you **started** your homework. (必须用过去式)

### 2️⃣ deny to do
- **结构**：deny + to do / deny + doing
- **用法**：表示“否认做过某事”，通常接动名词（doing）作宾语，但也可接不定式（to do），表示“拒绝做某事”。
- **例句**：
  - He **denied stealing** the money. (否认偷钱)
  - She **denied to help** him. (拒绝帮助他)

## 二、固定搭配/用法

- **spare time**：业余时间
  - 例句：I like to read in my spare time.
- **look for**：寻找（动作）
  - 例句：I am looking for my keys.
- **find**：找到（结果）
  - 例句：I found my keys under the sofa.
- **protective gear**：护具
  - 例句：Always wear protective gear when skating.
- **penalty box**：受罚席（体育）
  - 例句：The player was sent to the penalty box.
